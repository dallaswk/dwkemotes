Installation:

• In my Case using OpenIV,
• mods > update > x64 > dlcpacks > customanims > dlc.rpf > x64 > anim > ingame > clip_mp_.rpf
• Drag the .ycd files in the clip_mp_.rpf folder, to all of your other Anims


FavouriteAnims.xml:

• In my Case using OpenIV,
• menyooStuff > FavouriteAnims.xml
• be sure to have Edit Mode on, right Click on the XML and press Edit
• copy and paste the following Line into your FavouriteAnims.xml and save it


<Anim name="female_pose01_clip" dict="female_pose01@clyde"/>
<Anim name="female_pose02_clip" dict="female_pose02@clyde"/>
