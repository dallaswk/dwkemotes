-------- ♥ SP Installation to use Menyoo ♥ --------

Our Server noVa mods use the mod "addonprops" which you can find here https://www.gta5-mods.com/tools/addonprops

Download https://www.gta5-mods.com/tools/addonprops and install it according to instructions from this site
Put the .ycd files in your new addonprops folder and add our animations to your FavouriteAnims.xml (use the lines above)
